# Third-party data and attribution

The MIT license in this repository applies to the authors' code and original repository documentation only. It does **not** relicense third-party geodata, municipal imagery, standards, or manufacturer material.

## Stuttgart CityGML / municipal test data

Upstream source:

- Landeshauptstadt Stuttgart, Stadtmessungsamt — Open Geodata und Testdaten  
  https://www.stuttgart.de/leben/bauen/geoportal/open-data-und-testdaten

The City of Stuttgart page provides open geodata and test data, including a CityGML 3D city-model test dataset, and states that the listed datasets are supplied under open Creative Commons licenses. Users should consult the current upstream page for the license applying to the specific dataset at the time of reuse.

Historical analytical file in this repository:

- `data_raw/CityGML_Alles_2018_11_27_07_15_56.zip`

## LGL Baden-Württemberg Open GeoData

Upstream source:

- Landesamt für Geoinformation und Landentwicklung Baden-Württemberg (LGL)  
  https://www.lgl-bw.de/Produkte/Open-Data/

LGL states that its open geobasis data and geodata services may be used under **Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung – Version 2.0 (dl-de/by-2-0)** and specifies this attribution:

> Datenquelle: LGL, www.lgl-bw.de, dl-de/by-2-0

That attribution is preserved here and in the manuscript acknowledgments.
